Philosophy

We build.

Company

About Parasite Notify

Parasite Notify is a technology company that designs and operates its own products, rather than building for other companies. We start with a problem we think is worth solving properly, and we stay with it — from the first prototype through years of iteration.

We're early. Our products are still growing, and we'd rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise.

We work in small, focused teams that own their product end to end. Decisions about direction move slowly and carefully; decisions about implementation move fast. We'd rather ship something small and real than something large and imaginary.
